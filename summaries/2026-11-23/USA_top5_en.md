# USA Tech & AI Top 5 - 2026-11-23

1. **OpenAI releases GPT-5 preview with multimodal reasoning**
OpenAI unveiled a preview of GPT-5 focusing on multimodal reasoning across text, image, and audio. The model shows improved contextual understanding and reduced hallucinations in early benchmarks. Developers can access limited API keys for testing. The release signals a shift toward more integrated AI agents in enterprise workflows.

2. **Nvidia announces Blackwell GPU for AI training**
Nvidia launched the Blackwell architecture targeting large-scale AI training. With 2x performance per watt compared to Hopper, the GPU integrates advanced memory subsystems. Cloud providers announced immediate integration plans. The move intensifies competition in AI infrastructure.

3. **Google DeepMind demonstrates AI for protein folding**
DeepMind published results on AlphaFold 3 improvements for protein-ligand interactions. The system accelerates drug discovery timelines. Pharmaceutical partners reported 30% faster candidate screening. The breakthrough highlights AI's role in biotech.

4. **Apple integrates on-device AI into iOS 19**
Apple announced on-device AI models for real-time transcription and image generation. Privacy-focused processing keeps data local. Developers gain new APIs for contextual assistants. The update positions Apple as a leader in private AI.

5. **Anthropic releases Claude 4 with long context**
Anthropic launched Claude 4 with 1M token context window. Enterprises can process entire codebases in one prompt. Safety guardrails were updated for longer interactions. The release targets legal and research use cases.
